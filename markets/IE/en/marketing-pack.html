How to explain FIXON quality without empty claims

01 High-quality steel

Say: This model uses the material stated in its current product sheet and has a robust construction for professional work.

Show: Material, welds, adjustment, working elements and the actual product specification.

Avoid: Do not say that every FIXON product is stainless steel unless the specification confirms it.

02 Made in Warsaw

Say: FIXON develops and manufactures its tools in Warsaw, Poland.

Show: The brand, product construction and current company information.

Avoid: Do not turn the place of production into an unsupported technical performance claim.

03 Certificates and conformity

Say: We provide the documentation and conformity information required for the relevant product category.

Show: Only the current document that applies to the model.

Avoid: Do not use a general statement that every product has the same certificate.

04 Better than a general-purpose solution

Say: This tool is designed around a defined task, working range and method of control.

Show: The jaw, roller, guide, adjustment, tool weight or accessory that creates the difference.

Avoid: Do not claim that it is the best tool on the market without comparable evidence.

Visual checklist

  • Show the real FIXON product clearly and at a useful scale.
  • Show the material and work stage that the tool is designed for.
  • Use one main message and one action in each visual.
  • Use the correct model name and current product specification.
  • Keep hands, operators and loads in a credible and safe position.
  • Do not use a photograph of one model to advertise another model.
  • Do not hide the tool behind decoration, large text or an unrelated construction image.
  • Use the FIXON logo according to the supplied brand asset and leave clear space around it.
Before publication, check current prices, availability, delivery dates, specifications and required local information. Use the current FIXON product sheet for every technical claim.

How to explain FIXON quality without empty claims

01 High-quality steel

Say: This model uses the material stated in its current product sheet and has a robust construction for professional work.

Show: Material, welds, adjustment, working elements and the actual product specification.

Avoid: Do not say that every FIXON product is stainless steel unless the specification confirms it.

02 Made in Warsaw

Say: FIXON develops and manufactures its tools in Warsaw, Poland.

Show: The brand, product construction and current company information.

Avoid: Do not turn the place of production into an unsupported technical performance claim.

03 Certificates and conformity

Say: We provide the documentation and conformity information required for the relevant product category.

Show: Only the current document that applies to the model.

Avoid: Do not use a general statement that every product has the same certificate.

04 Better than a general-purpose solution

Say: This tool is designed around a defined task, working range and method of control.

Show: The jaw, roller, guide, adjustment, tool weight or accessory that creates the difference.

Avoid: Do not claim that it is the best tool on the market without comparable evidence.
