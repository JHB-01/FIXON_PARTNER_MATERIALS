1. Country and scope

The Supplier appoints the Distributor as exclusive general distributor for all FIXON products in the country stated above. Exclusivity comes from signing this agreement, not from selecting a price-list level or receiving a discount.

The ordinary Partner Agreement governs displays, stock support, training, materials, orders, prepayment and other duties. This agreement supplements it on exclusivity. It does not alter ownership of goods or the Partner's right to set resale prices independently.

2. One year and a further three years

The first exclusive term is one year, on the dates entered above. A further three years require written confirmation from both parties. Silence or continued ordering does not automatically renew exclusivity.

The continuation confirmation states the dates and agreed terms. The exclusive term and the 12-month validity of an earned discount level are separate rules.

3. Website and direct FIXON orders

The Supplier passes orders from the exclusive country placed through fixon.pro or directly with FIXON to the Distributor. There is no general exception allowing the Supplier to retain those orders for its own direct sales.

Before launch, the parties agree order routing, customer communication, necessary data, invoicing and transport, respecting customer rights and data-protection law. Routing an order does not create an unagreed commission or fee.

Service, complaints and technical support do not remove this rule. Where a customer contract already exists with FIXON, routing and fulfilment must respect that contract and any required consent; another party's obligations are not transferred automatically.

4. Purchase plan and monthly volume

The highest level granted in advance requires a signed commitment to further purchases at the agreed value for that level. The deadline is no later than three months after the first delivery. The initial trial order does not count.

After the first three months, at least EUR 25,000 in new net orders before discount is required each month. This is not a EUR 100,000 monthly purchase obligation. The dates entered above must clearly identify the first month being assessed.

The parties record new orders and values without counting previous periods again. Discount qualification through paid purchases in the preceding 30 days is separate from this monthly new-order commitment.

5. Discounts by product category

An agreed highest level granted in advance gives 35% for standard products and 40% for URBANROOT. Machines keep their separate table; they do not automatically receive 35% or 40%.

For road graders, BrukFill and LONCIN machines at the level from EUR 100,000, the rate is 15% for up to 20 machines and 20% for 21 or more. Count these machines together only in the current order. Other levels and offers follow the separate machine table in the Partner Agreement.

Discounts from different offers do not automatically add together. A 35% level earned through actual purchases does not require this agreement and does not by itself create exclusivity.

6. If the trial does not generate sales

For a trial linked to a purchase commitment, the Distributor may leave that commitment and return full-value goods covered by the trial if both conditions are met: sales in three months are below 20% of ordered net goods value before discount, and documented spending to sell FIXON is at least the first trial's net value before discount.

Advertising, fairs, displays and demonstrations may count; buying the goods themselves does not. FIXON pays return freight. Settlement uses the price actually paid for returned products under the returns procedure. There is no automatic penalty, unagreed restocking fee or unagreed discount clawback.

Ending the trial commitment requires records showing that the conditions were met and a documented settlement. The parties also confirm the implications for continuing exclusivity; a return must not be presented as automatic acceptance of unagreed new terms.

7. Independence and lawful market protection

The Distributor sets resale prices independently. Recommended retail prices and margin examples are not minimum or mandatory prices. A different lawful price alone does not justify removing exclusivity.

This agreement does not promise unlawful restrictions on third-party sales, unsolicited cross-border enquiries or effective internet use. Any permitted active-sales restriction must be separately described and checked for the market. Mandatory law takes precedence.

8. Review and termination

The parties review new orders, sales activity and customer service. They document and discuss discrepancies. Before signature, complete the effects of a volume shortfall, the correction process and early-termination terms below. Do not insert an automatic penalty or loss of paid goods.

Ending exclusivity does not itself decide whether the ordinary Partner Agreement ends. Paid orders, settlement, ownership, confidentiality and proper brand use follow the relevant agreement and applicable law.

9. Completion and signatures

Before signature, complete party details, country, dates, commitment value and deadline, the first review month and review and termination terms. This template does not confirm legal validity in every country. Check the chosen law and mandatory local rules for the specific arrangement.

Essential changes and the three-year continuation require written confirmation from both parties. Publishing an update does not replace consent.
