1. An extra strategic discount does not automatically add to R8 rates. The card must expressly confirm the rate and any combination. Publishing an update does not revoke existing signed rights.

2. The card identifies products, territory, channel, customers or project and dates. Rights must not be described more broadly than agreed or transferred without Supplier consent.

3. The Partner independently sets resale prices. A difference from the recommended retail price alone does not justify withdrawing a right. Market protection must not impose unlawful passive-sales or effective-internet-use restrictions.

4. If a separate exclusive general distribution agreement is signed, fixon.pro and direct FIXON orders from its country are passed to the distributor under that agreement. This card creates no conflicting general exception for Supplier direct sales. Technical and service support alone does not grant a right to retain those orders.

5. Complete activity, reporting and review terms below. A review may address actual breaches, missing agreed activity, stock discrepancies or loss of the grounds for the right. It must not be used to impose resale prices.

6. Restriction, suspension or withdrawal follows the Agreement and agreed card terms, with a documented notice to the Partner. It does not automatically change other rights or the Agreement itself. Essential changes require the appropriate agreement.

7. Approval requires agreed signatures or another express confirmation in a permitted form. A failure to object within seven days alone does not create a new obligation or revoke an existing signed right.
